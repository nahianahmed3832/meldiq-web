# Meldiq Privacy Policy

Prepared revision: `2026-11-04.1`. Effective on publication. This prepared copy must be checked against the release configuration and retention schedule before it replaces the live policy.

## Who is responsible

**GRIIPBUDDY LLC**, a Wyoming limited liability company, operates Meldiq. Contact us about privacy, access requests, or children's information at **contact@griipbuddy.com**, or write to **GRIIPBUDDY LLC, 1021 E Lincolnway, Cheyenne, WY 82001, Laramie County, United States**.

This policy covers Meldiq's websites and applications. It explains the information used to provide accounts, communities, messages, calls, and optional paid services. Independent payment providers, connected services, bots, and other third parties apply their own policies to their services.

## Information we process

- **Account and profile information:** your email, account identifiers, display name, username, settings, and optional profile information, photos, and links. You choose whether to provide optional details and their available visibility settings.
- **Authentication and security information:** password hashes, passkey public credentials, device public keys and certificates, sessions, verification and recovery records, and evidence needed to investigate account misuse. Keep your passwords, private keys, and recovery codes private.
- **Communications and activity:** messages and files handled by the features you use; conversation and community membership; delivery and read markers where enabled; reactions and activity information; call participation and connection metadata; and content you submit in a report. Encryption and visibility depend on the feature described below.
- **Technical information:** IP addresses, device or browser details, timestamps, request and security logs, service errors, and usage counts needed to deliver, secure, and maintain the service. Local storage and browser databases can retain preferences, signed-in device information, and cached communications on your device.
- **Optional services:** notification endpoint or device tokens if you enable notifications; service identifiers and permitted data if you connect an external account; and payment-provider references, purchase amounts, subscription status, and transaction records if you buy a product. Payment providers handle payment credentials in their payment flows.
- **Support information:** information you send to us and records needed to resolve your request. Do not send passwords, recovery material, or full payment credentials.

## Why we use information

We use information to create and secure accounts; deliver communications and notifications; show the profiles and communities you choose; operate permissions and moderation; process authorized purchases; prevent fraud and abuse; diagnose failures; answer requests; and meet legal obligations. We do not sell private messages or grant advertisers access to them.

Where a legal basis is required, processing needed to provide the service you request is based on performing our contract; security, reliability, and proportionate abuse prevention rely on legitimate interests where permitted; legal recordkeeping relies on legal obligations; and optional processing requiring consent relies on that consent. You may withdraw consent for optional processing without affecting prior lawful processing. A product permission, such as microphone access, does not authorize unrelated uses.

## Sharing and visibility

Other members receive the content you send and the profile information your audience settings allow. Community administrators and moderators can access information within their permissions and the reports submitted to them. Recipients can copy, export, capture, or share content outside Meldiq.

We use infrastructure and service providers for hosting, databases, storage, delivery, email, push notifications, and payment processing. Current infrastructure includes Hostinger, Supabase/PostgreSQL, Amazon Web Services, and Cloudflare. Prior MongoDB Atlas records and historical storage may be retained for recovery. Payment or connected-service providers process information when you use those features; older purchases can remain with their original billing provider. Google or Discord sign-in and profile connections share information according to the permissions shown in their authorization flows. Providers receive information needed for their service, subject to applicable arrangements and law.

We may disclose information when reasonably necessary to follow valid legal process, protect rights and safety, prevent abuse, or complete a corporate transaction with appropriate protections. We assess requests and apply applicable legal requirements. We do not promise that all information stays in one country: hosting, delivery networks, recovery copies, and external providers can process it in different countries. Required international-transfer safeguards must apply to the relevant processing.

## Encryption and its limits

Encryption in transit or at rest does not by itself prevent server access. Some Meldiq communication paths use device encryption; others allow server processing. **Current attachment delivery can include decryption keys readable by Meldiq's server**, so uploaded-file encryption is not a guarantee against server access. Account information, memberships, and routing metadata remain visible to the service.

Call-media protections depend on the call and participating clients; check the call's encryption indicator. A recipient, recording feature, screen share, bot, or integration can expose content to others. Meldiq has not completed an independent security audit. See the security page for the current feature-specific explanation, and choose what you share accordingly.

## Storage, retention, and deletion

We retain account information while your account is active and retain other information for the purposes described above, including security, recovery, dispute resolution, and legal or accounting duties. Different records have different retention rules; deleting an account or message is not a guarantee that every recipient copy, report, billing record, security log, or backup disappears immediately.

Current behavior includes delivery queues with an upper retention limit, files that can remain after a disappearing-message timer expires, retained recovery copies, and long-lived call and audit records. The release's detailed retention and account-deletion pages must state the actual periods and limitations; do not rely on a disappearing message to delete an associated file from storage. We will not represent unimplemented deletion or shorter retention as available.

Use the account export and deletion tools where offered. Contact us if you cannot access them or need assistance with a rights request. We may need proportionate verification to protect your account, and may retain information where law permits or requires it. We will explain a refusal or exception as required. Account deletion does not automatically cancel every external subscription; use the original provider's cancellation controls too.

## Your choices and rights

You can edit your profile and visibility settings, manage signed-in devices, change supported notification and activity preferences, disconnect services, and use available export or deletion tools. Browser and device settings control permissions such as notifications, camera, and microphone. Disabling a permission can prevent the related feature from working.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of personal information; object to or restrict certain processing; withdraw consent; or appeal a decision on a request. Contact contact@griipbuddy.com to exercise a right or ask about an authorized-agent request. We will respond within applicable legal deadlines, verify requests proportionately, and not unlawfully discriminate for exercising privacy rights. You may also complain to your relevant regulator. These statements do not imply that every jurisdiction's privacy law applies to GRIIPBUDDY LLC.

## Children and teenagers

Meldiq's minimum age is **13**, or a higher minimum required where you live. We do not permit children under 13 to create an account and do not knowingly collect their personal information for ordinary service use. If you believe a child under the applicable minimum age supplied information, contact contact@griipbuddy.com so we can investigate, restrict the account, and address the information as required by law. Do not send a child's sensitive documents through an unverified channel.

If you are a minor, get your parent's or guardian's permission as required by our Terms and applicable law. Think carefully before sharing a real name, photo, contact information, school, location, or other identifying details. A minimum-age checkbox is a declaration, not independent verification of a person's age.

## Website storage and changes

Essential cookies or browser storage may support sign-in, preferences, account switching, security, and offline behavior. Payment and external sign-in pages can use their own storage. Before adding optional analytics, advertising, or other processing that needs consent or additional notice, we must provide the required choice and accurate disclosures. This policy does not authorize undisclosed tracking.

We will update the revision date when this policy changes and provide notice of material changes as required. New uses requiring consent will not be treated as authorized merely because this policy was edited.
